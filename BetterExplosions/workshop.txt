Vehicles that go up properly. When a fuelled car, boat or motorbike (or a fuel pump) explodes, it doesn't just go dark: it blows apart, hurts whoever is too close, and leaves something worth looking at.

[h2]What an explosion does[/h2]
[list]
[*] A fireball, and vanilla's fuel pump blast and sound over the vehicle.
[*] The blast hurts: up to 60 health and shock within a couple of metres, falling to nothing at 8 m. Players flinch, cry out and see the red flash; infected and animals take more. (Vanilla's blast barely scratches a player.)
[*] Everything in the vehicle is badly damaged.
[*] Cars (Niva, Golf, Olga, Sarka, Humvee, M3S) become the burnt-out wreck of the same model and colour, with their cargo thrown out around them; a burnt wheel and the hood fly off at 45 degrees and land 8-10 m away.
[*] Boats lose their outboard motor - blown off the stern to splash down and sink - and the burnt, burst hull sinks where the boat was. Anything in the boat goes in the water.
[*] Motorbikes fly apart: wheels, fenders, exhaust and shields flung every which way.
[*] Wrecks and flying bits are gone at restart (the economy respawns vehicles as usual); thrown-out cargo stays like any dropped loot.
[/list]

[h2]What sets it off[/h2]
On its own, vanilla's explosives: a fuelled vehicle goes up a moment after
[list]
[*] a landmine goes off by it (or under it - drive onto one),
[*] an M79 40 mm explosive round hits it,
[*] C4 (or an IED with C4 in it) goes off by it,
[*] a hand grenade goes off right against it or under it.
[/list]
Afterwards it burns for a minute - stay out of the fire. Empty and wrecked vehicles don't go up.

With [b]Molotov[/b], a molotov fire reaching a fuelled vehicle sets it off too, and Molotov's spreading fire takes the place of ours.

DayZ Expansion's vehicles: add [b]Better Explosions - Expansion Vehicles[/b]. Big Machines' bulldozers, excavators and combines: add [b]Better Explosions - Big Machines[/b].

[h2]For modders[/h2]
Server side: BetterExplosions.Explode(vehicleOrPump) blows it up now; BetterExplosions.CanExplode(obj) says whether it would; BetterExplosions.BlastPeople(source, position) is just the blast's hit on the living. BetterExplosions.Event_OnExploded tells you whenever anything goes up; set BetterExplosions.s_OwnFire = false to light your own fire instead of ours. BetterExplosionsWreck.Add(carClass, wreckClass, turn) and BetterExplosionsWreck.Fling(carClass, pieceClass, onEdge) give more vehicles a wreck and burnt bits that fly off it. Source: https://github.com/jasonuithol/DayZBetterExplosions

[h2]For server owners[/h2]
[b]Install[/b] (paths relative to the server's install folder, shown as DayZServer/)
[olist]
[*] Put the mod's folder in the server's install folder as DayZServer/@BetterExplosions.
[*] Add it to the server's launch parameter (before any mod that uses it, like Molotov): -mod=...;@BetterExplosions
[*] Copy the key file DayZServer/@BetterExplosions/keys/tarbaby.bikey into DayZServer/keys/ if it isn't there already (Molotov and the Hand Truck mods use the same key).
[*] Restart the server.
[/olist]
Players only need to subscribe; the DayZ launcher loads the mod for them. Server and client.
