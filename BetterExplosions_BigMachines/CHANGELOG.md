# Changelog

## 0.1 - 2026-11-26

First release: Big Machines' machines that go up (Better Explosions) leave the wreck each
was restored from and fling burnt parts off it.

- Bulldozer: the bulldozer wreck, its track sprockets and burnt engine bits (radiator,
  exhaust, hoses, fan belt, control unit) flying
- Excavator: the digger wreck, its track sprockets and the same engine bits flying
- Combine harvester: the combine wreck, its wheels flying and its header thrown off the front
- A machine blown up where Big Machines put it doesn't lose its wreck: Big Machines keeps
  the map wreck it replaced sunk out of sight, sinking anything of that kind on that spot,
  so the new wreck is moved off the spot.
