Add-on for [b]Better Explosions[/b]: DayZ Expansion's vehicles go up like the vanilla ones. When a fuelled Expansion vehicle explodes, it's swapped for the matching vanilla wreck, burnt wheels, doors, bonnets and rotor blades fly off, and what was in its cargo is thrown out on the ground around it.

[list]
[*] Tractor: the tractor wreck
[*] Bus: the Ikarus bus wreck
[*] UAZ (every variant): the UAZ wreck
[*] Land Rover: Nasdara's Land Rover wreck
[*] UH-1H helicopter: the crashed Huey
[/list]
Other Expansion vehicles (Vodnik, Merlin, Little Bird, gyrocopters) have no matching wreck in the game; they still explode. Wrecks are gone at the next restart.

This used to be the Molotov - Expansion Vehicles add-on, which now just requires this one.

Requires: Better Explosions and DayZ-Expansion-Vehicles. Server and client.

[b]Install[/b] (paths relative to the server's install folder, shown as DayZServer/)
[olist]
[*] Put the mod's folder in the server's install folder as DayZServer/@BetterExplosions_Expansion.
[*] Add it to the server's launch parameter, after Better Explosions and the Expansion mods: -mod=...;@BetterExplosions;@BetterExplosions_Expansion
[*] Key: signed with the same key as Better Explosions (tarbaby.bikey); if DayZServer/keys/tarbaby.bikey is there already, there's nothing to copy.
[*] Restart the server.
[/olist]
Players only need to subscribe; the DayZ launcher loads the mod for them.
