# Changelog

## 0.1 - 2026-11-13

First release, moved out of the Molotov - Expansion Vehicles add-on (0.1): DayZ Expansion's
vehicles that go up (Better Explosions) leave the matching vanilla wreck and fling burnt
bits off it, like the vanilla cars do.

- Tractor: the tractor wreck
- Bus: the Ikarus bus wreck
- UAZ (every variant): the UAZ wreck
- Land Rover: Nasdara's Land Rover wreck
- UH-1H helicopter: the crashed Huey, its rotor blades flying
